Services

Two services, one team.

Every job is scoped to your site. Tell us what needs doing and we'll build the plan around it.

Construction cleaning

For builders, developers and project managers, from the first clean on site through to handover.

  • Builders' cleansDebris, dust and packaging cleared between trade stages.
  • Final cleansTop-to-bottom clean at practical completion.
  • Post-construction detailingPaint, adhesive, silicone and protective film removed.
  • Glass, windows & high-level dustingGlazing, frames, tracks, bulkheads and services.
  • Pre-handover presentation cleansA final touch-up before inspection or opening.

Gaps in the scope? We'll take care of them.

Removing floor protection, moving loose furniture, re-cleans after defects, urgent call-outs. If it falls between trade packages, hand it to us.

Commercial cleaning

For property managers and businesses that need their spaces kept to a consistent standard.

  • Offices & workplacesDaily, weekly or after-hours cleaning.
  • Retail & hospitalityFront-of-house and back-of-house, scheduled around trading.
  • Strata & common areasLobbies, lifts, stairwells, car parks and bin rooms.
  • Medical & educationClinics, schools and shared facilities.
  • Floors, windows & pressure washingPeriodic deep cleans on top of regular service.
